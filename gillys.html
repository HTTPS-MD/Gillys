About Gilly's

Four students, one shared complaint.

Gilly's started at the University of the East – Manila, where four students got tired of eating the same fixed meals, over and over, with no real say in what went into them.

So we built the ordering system we wished existed: one where every ingredient is a switch, every macro is visible before checkout, and no one has to eat around a component they don't want.
